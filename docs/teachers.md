# Teacher outreach list

Potential early Realia users — independent language teachers who run
lessons over video call (Google Meet, Zoom) and would plausibly want
shared draggable objects for teaching vocabulary/grammar live. Not a
CRM, just a working list to seed outreach.

## Target profile

- Teaches 1-to-1 and/or small groups over video call
- Markets directly (own brand/Instagram/WhatsApp), not just through a
  marketplace like italki/Preply — easier to reach and pitch directly
- Lower-level groups (A1/A2/B1) benefit most from visual/object-based
  vocabulary teaching
